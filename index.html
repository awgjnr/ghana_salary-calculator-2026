How to read this
“Retention” is net salary ÷ gross salary. “Other employee deductions” covers mandatory employee-side items such as social security, UIF, NHF, SHIF or housing levy depending on the country. Relief-toolkit cards below show where more claims are commonly available if the employee qualifies.
